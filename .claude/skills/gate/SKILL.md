---
name: gate
description: Interview-style check on what I just built. Use when I type /gate or finish a session or milestone.
---

1. Look at what changed this session (recent commits and the current diff) and PROGRESS.md.
2. Ask me 2 questions (3 if I've just finished a milestone) like a senior backend interviewer
   at a Warsaw product company would. Questions must be about decisions in MY code
   ("why a unique constraint and not a check in the service?"), plus one wider
   "what happens at scale / when it fails" question.
3. Ask one question at a time. Wait for my answer.
4. Grade each answer: strong / acceptable / weak, with one sentence on what a strong
   answer would add. Be honest; don't soften.
5. If any answer was weak, add it to "Open questions" in PROGRESS.md.
